Home screen — composition test

The concept fixes the mechanics of the new home screen — a fixed news carousel fed from q2_community_content, and below it a free 12-column dashboard the user arranges themselves. It deliberately leaves one question open, because it is the one that is decided by looking at it rather than by arguing: how much of the screen does the news zone get? These three variants differ in that dimension and in nothing else.

  • Today's home screen is a placeholder. HeroPanel shows the active installation with four carousel dots wired to nothing, and the grid below it is really the roadmap: one card per module, planned ones badged "planned".
  • The hero and the news want the same space. The concept resolves it — the news hero replaces HeroPanel entirely, and the active installation stays where it already is, in the action bar and the installation rail.
  • v1 has exactly two dashboard modules (playtime & statistics, config profiles), so every variant shows the same two tiles at the same 6×5 cells. The free space around them is not a mock-up shortcut — that is what v1 looks like.
  • Every variant is drawn at real chrome sizes: titlebar 68 px, rail 76 px, action bar 96 px, window 1280×820, grid 12 columns × 40 px rows.

All three keep the concept's contracts unchanged: index.json decides which entry is visible, when and in which order; the .md carries the template, the content and up to three external-link buttons; images are fetched and cached by main and served over q2launcher://; the grid stays 12 columns × 40 px rows with an explicit arrange mode. Only the proportion changes.

Every variant is interactive without JavaScript: the slide pills and the carousel's dots and arrows switch between the three templates, offline · no feed shows the built-in welcome slide with its stale note, and arrange mode turns on the cell grid, the drag grips, the resize handles, the catalog bar and the live-region announcement.
