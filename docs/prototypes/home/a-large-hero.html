PLAY

What it gives

  • The news actually land — a split slide gets an image big enough to read and a headline at 30 px.
  • Closest to the reference the concept names; the launcher feels curated rather than administrative.
  • A 5-row tile still fits fully, so the two v1 modules are complete without scrolling.

What it costs

  • The dashboard is a strip, not a canvas — a second row of tiles is always below the fold, which fights the "build your own screen" promise.
  • Half the main area is permanently spent on content the user cannot configure.
  • With only two v1 modules the lower half looks thin and the upper half looks dominant.
