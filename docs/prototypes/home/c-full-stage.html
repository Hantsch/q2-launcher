PLAY

What it gives

  • The strongest news presence of the three — a split slide gets a real image and three lines of body text, and banner finally has room.
  • Once scrolled, the dashboard is the biggest canvas of the three variants (~13 rows), because it does not share the viewport with the hero.
  • The two zones never fight for space; each gets a full screen.

What it costs

  • The dashboard is invisible by default — a customisable home screen the user has to scroll to find is one most users will never customise.
  • Arrange mode only makes sense after scrolling, so the interaction has a hidden precondition.
  • The main area becomes scrollable, which the shell's three fixed zones deliberately avoided so far.
  • Worst fit for the concept's vision sentence: "the bottom is theirs" — here the bottom is off-screen.
